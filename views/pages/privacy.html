Privacy Notice

Effective August 1, 2026. This notice explains how {{siteName}} handles information submitted through this wholesale website, retailer CRM, order system, and marketing tools.

Information we collect

We collect information needed to evaluate and serve licensed Washington cannabis businesses, including business and trade names, contact names, business email addresses and telephone numbers, license and UBI numbers, delivery addresses, product interests, order details, communications, follow-up tasks, campaign consent, and unsubscribe status.

We also maintain technical records needed to secure and operate the service, such as consent timestamps, hashed request fingerprints, authentication activity, error logs, and audit records. We do not collect payment-card information through this website.

How we use information

We use information to verify business eligibility, respond to inquiries, process wholesale order requests, coordinate lawful fulfillment, maintain account and order history, provide customer service, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, and improve the website and operations.

Marketing email is optional. Marketing consent is separate from placing an order or submitting a business inquiry. Recipients may unsubscribe at any time. An unsubscribe does not stop transactional communications about an existing order or account.

CRM and public business information

The CRM may contain publicly advertised business information, commercially licensed business-directory information, information supplied directly by a retailer, and license-verification information. The source and source date are recorded where available. A shop's presence in the CRM does not automatically authorize bulk marketing.

Service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, databases, product-image storage, email delivery, security, and analytics. Current platform providers may include Railway, PostgreSQL hosting, Cloudinary, and Resend. Providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform their services and are expected to protect it.

Retention

We retain order, license-verification, fulfillment, tax, audit, and compliance records for the period required by applicable law and legitimate business needs. Business inquiries, prospect records, and inactive CRM contacts are reviewed periodically and may be deleted or archived when no longer needed. Consent and unsubscribe records may be retained to document compliance and prevent unwanted marketing.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, secure administrator sessions, rate limits, audit records, database controls, and restricted administrative pages. No system is completely secure. Suspected incidents are investigated and notifications are made when required by applicable law.

Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with you, or request a marketing opt-out. We may need to verify your identity and authority to act for the business. Some records cannot be deleted when retention is required for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.
