Source control

License verification is not the same as lead-list permission.

Washington LCB identifies Active and Pending (Issued) as currently active statuses. The LCB public-records page also warns that records received through the Public Records Act may not be used for commercial purposes and notes possible list errors.

The CRM records the source, source date, usage basis, and import batch for every refresh. License status can be verified without automatically treating every public-record entry as a marketing subscriber.
